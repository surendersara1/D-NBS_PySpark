==========================================================================
  18 - approx_count_distinct
==========================================================================
  exact distinct cannot be partially aggregated before the shuffle. HyperLogLog sketches can.

--- identical here because the set is tiny - at 900M rows the cost is not ---
+-----+-------------------+---------------+
|exact|approx_default_5pct|approx_1pct_rsd|
+-----+-------------------+---------------+
|7    |7                  |7              |
+-----+-------------------+---------------+

At scale: exact distinct ships every raw value across the network.
approx ships a fixed-size sketch per partition. That is the whole difference.
