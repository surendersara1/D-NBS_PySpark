==========================================================================
  03 - cast
==========================================================================
  change type. What a BAD value does depends on ANSI mode - read the note below, it bites people moving Spark versions.

--- double -> int / string / decimal ---
+--------+----------+---+---------+-------+---------+
|order_id|unit_price|qty|price_int|qty_str|price_dec|
+--------+----------+---+---------+-------+---------+
|ORD-1001|4.5       |3  |4        |3      |4.50     |
|ORD-1001|249.99    |1  |249      |1      |249.99   |
|ORD-1002|39.0      |2  |39       |2      |39.00    |
|ORD-1003|899.0     |1  |899      |1      |899.00   |
+--------+----------+---+---------+-------+---------+

ansi.enabled = true
--- try_cast - NULL on failure, in every Spark version ---
+------------+--------+
|v           |try_cast|
+------------+--------+
|42          |42      |
|not_a_number|NULL    |
|NULL        |NULL    |
+------------+--------+

--- cast with ANSI off - the silent NULL. This is how bad data disappears. ---
+------------+-------------+
|v           |cast_ansi_off|
+------------+-------------+
|42          |42           |
|not_a_number|NULL         |
|NULL        |NULL         |
+------------+-------------+

cast with ANSI on  -> NumberFormatException - CAST_INVALID_INPUT. The job FAILS instead of hiding the row.
