  05 - filter / where
==========================================================================
  the same method under two names. Pick one.

--- filter: region == "NA" ---
+--------+------+------------+
|order_id|region|order_status|
+--------+------+------------+
|ORD-1001|NA    |completed   |
|ORD-1002|NA    |refunded    |
|ORD-1004|NA    |refunded    |
|ORD-1006|NA    |pending     |
|ORD-1009|NA    |completed   |
+--------+------+------------+

--- AND -> &   (each side in its own brackets) ---
+--------+------+------------+
|order_id|region|order_status|
+--------+------+------------+
|ORD-1005|EMEA  |completed   |
|ORD-1007|EMEA  |completed   |
|ORD-1011|EMEA  |completed   |
+--------+------+------------+

--- isin ---
+--------+------------+
|order_id|order_status|
+--------+------------+
|ORD-1002|refunded    |
|ORD-1004|refunded    |
|ORD-1010|cancelled   |
+--------+------------+

--- between (inclusive on both ends) ---
+--------+----------+---+
|order_id|sku       |qty|
+--------+----------+---+
|ORD-1001|SKU-EL-201|1  |
|ORD-1002|SKU-AP-410|2  |
|ORD-1003|SKU-EL-115|1  |
|ORD-1005|SKU-BE-330|2  |
+--------+----------+---+

orders where customer_email == None : 0  <- always 0
orders where customer_email isNull(): 1  <- correct
